# Stakeholder Collaboration & Work Methods

This document defines how Candidates, Recruiters (Independent/Agency), and Companies interact within the platform to ensure a seamless and high-value experience.

## 1. The Interaction Model

The platform operates as a **Trust Proxy**, facilitating communication while enforcing technical and financial guardrails.

### A. Async-First Communication
- **Standard:** Initial interactions are asynchronous.
- **Tools:** AI-led screening, automated speech analysis, and technical audits happen without direct human intervention.
- **Goal:** To minimize "interview fatigue" and scheduling friction.

### B. Sync-Second (Final Stage)
- **Standard:** Direct human-to-human interaction is reserved for the final culture fit and offer stages.
- **Workflow:** Once a candidate is in the "Shortlist" (verified by AI and the Recruiter), the platform facilitates a direct meeting via integrated video conferencing.

## 2. Collaboration Flows by Actor

### 🏢 Companies vs. 🕵️ Recruiters
- **Methods:**
    - **Collaborative Filtering:** Companies set the "Budget & Tech Bar"; Recruiters hunt and submit talent into that predefined "Bucket".
    - **Feedback Loops:** Recruiters receive real-time insights on why their candidates were rejected (e.g., "English score too low for this role").

### 🕵️ Recruiters vs. 👨‍💻 Candidates
- **Methods:**
    - **Advocacy:** Recruiters act as agents for the candidate, helping them prepare for the AI Interviewer.
    - **Transparency:** Candidates see which recruiter "discovered" them and their current status in the pipeline.

### 👨‍💻 Candidates vs. 🏢 Companies
- **Methods:**
    - **Value Discovery:** Candidates demonstrate value through proof-of-work (GitHub/Speech) before the company's first human touch.
    - **Lifecycle Support:** Post-hire, the platform acts as the bridge for payments, taxes, and mobility support.

## 3. Work Methods & Rules of Engagement

| Activity | Primary Method | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sourcing | Passive (AI) / Active (Recruiter) | Platform / Recruiter |
| Tech Auditing | Automated (Background) | AI Engine |
| Interviewing | AI Interviewer -> Final Client Call | Candidate / Company |
| Negotiation | In-Platform (Auction Dynamic) | Candidate / Recruiter |
| Compliance | Automated (Escrow/DARF) | Platform |

## 4. Nuance: "The Success Loop"

The ecosystem is a self-reinforcing circle:
1.  **Recruiters** find talent -> 2. **AI** audits quality -> 3. **Platform** ensures tax safety -> 4. **Companies** hire with confidence -> 5. **Candidates** build careers and refer others.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **No-Poaching Policy:** The platform enforces a non-compete/non-circumvention layer. Recruiters and Companies agree to process all hiring actions through the platform to ensure the **Success Fee** and **Retention Reward** are protected.
