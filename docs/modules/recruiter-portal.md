# Recruiter Portal: Agencies & Independent Headhunters

This module empowers professional recruiters and agencies to scale their hunting operations and manage their earnings within the platform.

## 1. Professional Recruiter Personas

- **Independent Headhunter:** Freelance recruiters focusing on niche technical roles.
- **Recruitment Agency:** Teams with hierarchical access (Manager/Member) to manage multiple clients and pipelines.

## 2. Advanced Hunting Tools (CRM)

- **AI-Powered Search:** Natural language search across the verified global candidate pool (e.g., "Find me Elixir developers with high English fluency and low salary expectation").
- **Pipeline Management:** A dedicated CRM to track candidate status from "Identified" to "Hired".
- **Candidate Submission:** Ability to "Bring your own talent" into the platform to use the AI auditing tools and close the hire with legal safety.

## 3. Commission & Earnings Management

The platform automates the financial lifecycle of the recruiter:

- **Success Fee Split:** Automatic split of the company's payment between the platform and the recruiter (e.g.,Recruiter receives 10-15% of the candidate's annual salary).
- **Earnings Dashboard:** Real-time tracking of:
    - **Pending Commissions:** Funds held in Escrow awaiting milestone completion.
    - **Available Funds:** Credits ready for withdrawal to their local bank account.
    - **Performance Metrics:** Placement rate, time-to-hire, and quality of hire (AI-audited).
- **Automated Invoicing:** The platform automatically generates the tax documents (Invoice/NF) required for the recruiter to receive their commission legally.

## 4. Recruiter Marketing & Attribution Tools

To scale sourcing operations, recruiters can generate branded assets directly from the platform:

- **Custom Landing Pages:** Generate job-specific or recruiter-specific landing pages with custom headers, call-to-actions, and tracking scripts.
- **Social Media Snippets:** Create optimized "Share Cards" and snippets for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and WhatsApp that include unique tracking IDs.
- **Attribution Engine:**
    - **Tracking Codes:** Every link generated contains a unique attribution ID (Source, Recruiter, Campaign).
    - **Origin Reports:** Real-time dashboard showing where candidates are coming from (Organic, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, etc.).
    - **Performance Metrics:** Conversion rates per channel and ROI for each marketing campaign.

## 5. Retention Reward (90-Day Quality Bonus)

To ensure quality over quantity, the platform implements a configurable **Retention Reward**:

- **The Milestone:** When a hired candidate completes **90 days (3 months)** of active work with the company.
- **Configurable Payout:** The company can set a specific "Retention Bonus" amount during the job posting (e.g., $500 or 5% of the success fee).
- **Escrow Logic:** This value is held in the **Smart Wallet Escrow** from day 1 and is released automatically to the recruiter's wallet once the 90-day webhook is triggered.
- **Recruiter Badge:** Recruiters with a high "Retention Rate" (90%+) receive a **Quality Badge**, granting them priority in candidate submissions.

## 6. Nuance: "The Hunting Economy"

Recruiters are incentivized to find "Top Efficiency" candidates (high E-Score). Higher E-Score placements result in better rankings for the recruiter's profile, giving them access to exclusive high-budget job postings from top global companies.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Identity Verification:** Just like candidates, professional recruiters must undergo KYC/Face Recognition before withdrawing their first commission to ensure financial security.
