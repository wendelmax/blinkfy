# Global Mobility & Equity Management

This module provides the logistics and legal framework for developers to move between countries and participate in company ownership (Stock Options) across borders.

## 1. Global Mobility & Relocation (Stage 2 Support)

Many developers start remote with the goal of eventually relocating. The platform automates this transition:

### A. Visa Concierge (AI-Assisted)
- **Eligibility Check:** IA assesses the candidate's profile against visa requirements for specific countries (e.g., H1-B for USA, Blue Card for Germany, Tech Visa for Portugal).
- **Document Templating:** Automated generation of required letters of intent, employment contracts tailored for immigration, and translation management.
- **Relocation Packages:** Integration with vendors for physical moving services, housing search (Digital Nomad friendly), and local tax registration (Tax ID acquisition).

## 2. International Equity Management (ESOP/RSU)

Granting stock options to employees in countries like Brazil or Mexico is legally complex. We simplify it:

- **Phantom Shares & RSUs:** Support for "Phantom" equity models that mimic stock performance without the immediate legal complexity of cross-border share issuance.
- **Vesting Automation:** Integration with platforms like **Carta** or **Pulley** to track vesting periods and automatically update the developer's "Net Wealth" dashboard.
- **Tax-Safe Vesting:** The deduction engine automatically calculates the "Benefit-in-Kind" tax owed when shares vest or options are exercised, preventing tax surprises.

## 3. Referral & Reputation Network

A circular economy of talent:

- **Verified Referrals:** Candidates can refer other developers. The "Referrer Reputation" increases if the referred candidate passes the AI screening.
- **Monetized Referrals:** A success fee (referral bonus) is automatically processed and held in the Smart Wallet when the new hire starts.
- **Global Talent Badge:** Top 1% candidates become "Ambassadors", giving them early access to highly competitive roles.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Vesting Nuance:** For LATAM hires, we recommend 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff as the standard to align with global startup benchmarks while maintaining local legal safety.
